Quick answers

Before you hit record

What exactly do I send you?

Your raw recording — audio file, video file, or both — uploaded to the shared folder we create for your show after checkout. If you record on Riverside, Zoom or a similar tool, send the separate speaker tracks if you have them; if not, the combined file works too.

How fast is “72 hours,” really?

The clock starts when your raw files land in the folder. Standard delivery is within 72 hours per episode, as shown in the sample package above. If an episode needs something unusual (heavy repair, a guest’s missing track), we tell you before the clock becomes a problem.

Is this a subscription?

No automatic subscription. Checkout charges the first month as one single payment. After that, the service continues month to month when you renew — no long-term contract and nothing charged without you checking out again.

I don’t have a podcast yet. Can I start with the pilot?

Yes — the $250 pilot is built for exactly that. Record one episode the simple way, and we return it fully produced so you can hear and see what your show sounds like before committing to a monthly plan.

What if I record more or fewer than 4 episodes a month?

The plans are built around a weekly show (4 episodes). Record fewer and the remaining production slots roll to the next episode batch within the same month cycle; talk to us after checkout if your cadence is different and we’ll align the plan to it.

Do you work with Spanish-language shows?

Yes. Editing, captions and show notes are available in English and Spanish — useful if your audience is bilingual or your show runs in Spanish.
